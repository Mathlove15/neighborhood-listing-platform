# Neighborhood Listing Platform: Value Proposition

**Team:** Team A — Aster
**Course:** CIS 3610
**Prepared by:** Charles Braden

## Who We Serve

Our platform serves property professionals preparing neighborhood listings,
people searching for local housing, and small businesses seeking visibility
within their community.

## The Problem

In our simulated discovery scenario, property professionals move listing
information between spreadsheets and separate portals. They need time to
prepare accurate details and review information before publication.

Local sponsors have limited design resources and need an easy way to
create promotions and check business details before submitting them.
People browsing listings need a clear mobile experience and useful filters.

## Our Proposed Value

The Neighborhood Listing Platform brings property discovery and local
business promotions into one neighborhood-focused experience.

Property professionals can prepare listing drafts and submit information
for review. Housing seekers can browse mobile-friendly listings and narrow
results by price and neighborhood. Sponsors can enter business information
into a structured promotional template and preview the resulting card.

## Benefits

- Property professionals can preserve unfinished work and correct listing
  information before publication.
- Housing seekers can find relevant listings through readable layouts
  and focused search controls.
- Local businesses can prepare consistent promotional cards without
  needing graphic design software.

## Why This Approach

Our proposed distinction is the combination of neighborhood housing
discovery, simple listing preparation, and local sponsor promotions.
The design emphasizes clear forms, review steps, and mobile usability.

## Week 8 MVP

The planned MVP includes listing drafts, listing review, mobile-friendly
views, sponsor templates, sponsor previews, and price/neighborhood filters.

Automatic photo compression and property inquiry submission are deferred.
The MVP plan describes intended scope; it does not mean every feature
has already been implemented.

## How We Will Evaluate Value

Future usability checks will examine whether users can save and restore
a draft, complete a listing review, find listings with filters, and create
and correct a sponsor preview. Mobile checks will examine readability,
horizontal overflow, and primary touch targets.

These are proposed evaluation activities, not completed research results.

## Evidence and Assumptions

This value proposition is based on the fictional scenarios and simulated
notes in `docs/discovery-package.md`. Benefits remain hypotheses to test
with users. The current prototype demonstrates browser-local draft
persistence; it does not establish real-world demand or business outcomes.
